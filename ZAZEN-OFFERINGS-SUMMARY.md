## Core Technology Stack

### Hardware Layer
- **BLDC Variable-Speed Compressor**: 2000-3500 RPM modulation, 25-40% energy savings
- **EV Traction Inverter (5kW)**: Automotive-grade motor drive
- **Electronic Expansion Valve (EEV)**: 500-pulse precision control
- **R290 Natural Refrigerant**: GWP=3 (vs R134a=1,430), 10-15% better efficiency
- **48V DC Bus Architecture**: Solar/battery integration, hybrid power flexibility
- **Astra VCU Master Controller**: Automotive-qualified Renesas MCU with CAN bus
- **Nayana 4.3" HMI Display**: Professional touchscreen with real-time dashboards

### Intelligence Layer
**Cloud Intelligence (Gemini-Powered)**:
- 2-4 week failure prediction across all components
- Solar forecast sync for renewable energy optimization
- Grid-demand response for peak-shaving
- Cross-fleet learning from 10,000+ connected units
- Automated compliance report generation

**Edge Intelligence (On-Device AI)**:
- Thermal inertia modeling (learns product mass, ambient, door access)
- Vibration anomaly detection (bearing wear, mounting issues)
- EEV precision tuning (±0.5°C superheat control)
- Adaptive defrost scheduling (frost-buildup monitoring)
- Route-based pre-loading (GPS-aware thermal management)
