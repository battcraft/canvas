### 4. Writing Guidelines

**Voice & Tone**:
- Storytelling approach = 8/10 (data-driven narrative)
- Transparency = 9/10 (include challenges, not just wins)
- Confidence = 7/10 (let customer results speak, not hype)
- Technical depth = 6/10 (accessible to business buyers)

**Narrative Techniques**:
- **Arc**: Problem (rising tension) → Solution (intervention) → Results (resolution) → Insight (wisdom)
- **Specificity**: Use exact numbers, dates, locations, names
- **Contrast**: Before/after comparison tables for visual impact
- **Humanity**: Include customer quotes in first person
- **Surprise**: Lead with counterintuitive finding in "Insight" section

**Quantification Requirements**:
- Every claim must have a number (%, ₹, hours, incidents)
- Use ranges when exact is confidential (₹2-3L instead of ₹2.4L)
- Compare to baseline ("37% reduction vs. pre-ZAZEN consumption")
- Show calculation ("25 locations × ₹12,400 savings/month = ₹3.1L/year")

### 5. Length Guidelines

- **Full case study**: 1800-2500 words
- **Short version** (for PDF 1-pager): 800-1000 words
- **Social media snippet**: 150-200 words with key metric
- **Email teaser**: 100-150 words with CTA to full case study

### 6. Quality Checklist

Before delivering:
- [ ] Customer approved all quotes and metrics
- [ ] Verified all numbers against source data (invoices, logs, reports)
- [ ] Included before/after comparison table with 5+ metrics
- [ ] Named specific product models deployed (ZF-500M, not "ZAZEN unit")
- [ ] Disclosed ROI breakeven period (18 months, not "fast payback")
- [ ] Included unexpected insight or learning (not just expected benefits)
- [ ] Added "Looking Forward" section (expansion, advice for peers)
- [ ] Customer quote uses first person ("we discovered" not "they discovered")
- [ ] Removed promotional language (no "amazing", "incredible", "revolutionary")
- [ ] Read aloud test passed (sounds like customer telling their own story)

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Vague outcomes**: "Saved money" → ✅ "₹3.1 lakh annual savings (37% energy reduction)"

❌ **Omitting challenges**: Only success story → ✅ Include "Implementation took 2 weeks longer due to site access constraints"

❌ **Promotional tone**: "ZAZEN is amazing!" → ✅ "The predictive maintenance system flagged..."

❌ **Missing timeline**: "Saved energy" → ✅ "After 18 months, achieved 37% savings with ROI breakeven in month 19"

❌ **No customer voice**: All third-person → ✅ Include 2-3 first-person quotes from stakeholders
