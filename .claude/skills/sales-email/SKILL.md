# Sales Email Skill

## Purpose

Generate personalized, high-conversion outreach emails that speak directly to ICP-specific pain points with relevant proof points and low-friction calls to action.

## When to Use

- Cold outreach to new prospects (first touch)
- Warm follow-ups after initial contact (nurture sequences)
- Post-demo follow-up with ROI calculations
- Re-engagement campaigns for dormant leads
- Partnership and distribution channel outreach

### 2. Email Types & Structures

#### Type A: Cold Outreach (First Touch)

```
Subject: [Specific pain point] at [Company Name]?

Hi [First Name],

[OPENER: Personalized observation about their company/industry]
Example: "I noticed ABC Restaurant expanded to 25 locations in the past year—congratulations on the growth."

[PAIN POINT: Specific challenge for this ICP with quantified impact]
Example: "Most QSR chains at your scale are bleeding ₹8-12 lakh annually on cold chain inefficiencies that remain invisible without real-time monitoring."

[PROOF POINT: One relevant metric from similar customer]
Example: "Similar 25-location chain recently uncovered 37% energy waste from thermostat drift—saved ₹3.1 lakh/year after ZAZEN retrofit."

[SOFT CTA: Low-friction next step]
Example: "Worth a 15-minute conversation to see if similar patterns exist in your cold chain?"

Best,
[Your Name]
[Title] | ZAZEN SYSTEMS
[Phone] | [Email]
```

**Length**: 100-150 words (4-5 short paragraphs)

#### Type B: Warm Follow-up (Nurture Sequence)

```
Subject: Following up: [Their specific pain point mentioned in prior conversation]

Hi [First Name],

Following up on our [date] conversation about [specific issue they mentioned].

[RECAP: What they told you about their situation]
Example: "You mentioned the 14 freezers at your Mumbai locations are 8+ years old and you're seeing increased maintenance calls."

[NEW INSIGHT: Additional data point or perspective]
Example: "Freezers in that age range typically have 40-50% efficiency degradation vs. new—but full replacement at ₹2.5L+ per unit is often hard to justify."

Example: "ZAZEN retrofit (₹65K per unit) brings 8-year-old freezers to EV-grade performance at 25% of replacement cost—18-month payback in most cases."

Best,
[Your Name]
```

**Length**: 120-180 words (5-6 short paragraphs)

### 3. Writing Guidelines

**Voice & Tone**:
- Formality = 6/10 (professional but conversational)
- Confidence = 7/10 (helpful expert, not pushy salesperson)
- Urgency = 5/10 (problem-focused, not artificial scarcity)
- Personalization = 9/10 (specific to their company, not generic template)

**Subject Line Rules**:
- ✅ Specific to their situation: "[Pain point] at [Company Name]?"
- ✅ Intrigue with metrics: "₹8.4L hidden energy drain in 25-location QSR chains"
- ✅ Question format: "How does [Competitor] maintain cold chain at 50+ locations?"
- ❌ Generic hype: "Revolutionary freezer technology"
- ❌ ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation: "SAVE MONEY NOW!!!"
- ❌ Overly long: Keep under 60 characters

**Opening Line Requirements**:
- Must reference their company specifically (location count, recent news, geographic presence)
- Research sources: LinkedIn, company website, news articles, Crunchbase
- Bad generic: "I hope this email finds you well"
- Good specific: "I noticed ABC Restaurant expanded to Tier 2 cities in Q3"

**Pain Point Articulation**:
- Lead with ICP-specific pain (not product features)
- Quantify impact in ₹ amounts or hours wasted
- Use "most [ICP segment]" framing: "Most QSR chains at 25+ locations..."
- Reference industry data: "According to NABARD cold chain study..."

**Proof Points**:
- One relevant metric from similar customer (anonymized if needed)
- Specific numbers: "37% savings" not "significant savings"
- Timeline context: "After 18 months" not "eventually"
- Comparable scale: If they have 25 locations, cite 20-30 location example

**Call to Action (CTA)**:
- Low friction: 15-20 minute call, not "schedule demo"
- Offer value: "ROI calculator" not "learn about ZAZEN"
- Provide optionality: "Tuesday 2pm or Thursday 11am?" (not "let me know when")
- Make it easy: Calendar link, phone number, reply email

### 5. Email Sequence Cadence

**Cold Outreach Sequence** (7 touches over 21 days):
1. **Day 1**: Initial outreach (pain point + proof point)
2. **Day 4**: Case study share (similar customer story)
3. **Day 8**: Educational content (blog post on industry challenge)
4. **Day 12**: Different pain point angle (e.g., compliance if energy didn't resonate)
5. **Day 16**: Soft breakup ("Should I close your file?")
6. **Day 21**: Final value offer (ROI calculator, industry report)
7. **Day 45**: Long-term nurture (re-engage with new product/feature)

**Post-Demo Nurture** (5 touches over 30 days):
1. **Day 1**: Thank you + ROI analysis
2. **Day 3**: Answer objection (e.g., installation timeline concern)
3. **Day 7**: Customer testimonial (similar ICP)
4. **Day 14**: Pilot proposal (reduce commitment risk)
5. **Day 30**: Final push (time-bound incentive if applicable)

### 6. Quality Checklist

Before sending:
- [ ] Subject line under 60 characters, specific to their situation
- [ ] Personalized opener references their company specifically
- [ ] Pain point articulated with quantified impact (₹ or hours)
- [ ] One relevant proof point from similar customer
- [ ] Low-friction CTA with specific next step
- [ ] Length under 150 words (cold) or 200 words (warm)
- [ ] No generic phrases ("I hope this finds you well", "reaching out")
- [ ] Read aloud test: sounds conversational, not templated
- [ ] Removed filler words: "just", "really", "actually", "basically"
- [ ] Proofread for typos and grammar

Let me know if [Tuesday 3pm] or [Thursday 11am] works to discuss pilot logistics.

Amit

**Why this works**:
- ✅ Recalls specific pain point from their demo (Mumbai location)

- ✅ Reduces commitment risk (pilot 3 locations, not full 25)
- ✅ Specific timeline (install next week, 30-day trial)
- ✅ Provides calendar options

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Generic opening**: "I hope this email finds you well" → ✅ "Noticed ABC expanded to Pune last quarter"

❌ **Feature dumping**: "ZAZEN has CAN bus, EEV, IoT monitoring..." → ✅ "Discovered 37% energy waste, saved ₹3.1L/year"

❌ **Vague CTA**: "Let me know if interested" → ✅ "Tuesday 2pm or Thursday 11am for 15-min ROI walkthrough?"

❌ **No proof point**: "We can save you money" → ✅ "Similar 25-location chain saved ₹3.1L annually (37% reduction)"

❌ **Too long**: 300+ word email → ✅ Keep under 150 words for cold outreach

❌ **Pushy urgency**: "Limited time offer!" → ✅ "Most QSR chains lose ₹8-12L annually to invisible inefficiencies"
