# ZAZEN SYSTEMS Product Portfolio & Positioning

## Portfolio Overview

ZAZEN SYSTEMS offers a comprehensive range of intelligent cold chain solutions spanning two primary product lines:
1. **Premium New Freezers** - Complete units with built-in intelligence
2. **Smart Retrofit Systems** - Upgrade kits for existing equipment

Each product line features multiple SKUs tailored to different market segments, applications, and budgets.
